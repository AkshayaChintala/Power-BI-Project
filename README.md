# E-Commerce Sales, Customer &amp; Product Analytics | Power BI

## Project Overview
The E-Commerce Sales, Customer & Product Analytics project is a Business Intelligence (BI) project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze an e-commerce company's sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, and order trends across multiple cities in India.

The main objective of this project is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, and interactive dashboards.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance, revenue, orders, and monthly sales trends.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour and buying patterns.
- Identify new, repeat, and high-value customers based on their purchases.
- Evaluate product performance based on revenue and quantity sold.
- Identify the best-performing and low-performing product categories.
- Monitor delivered, cancelled, and pending orders.

## Tools and Technologies
- **Microsoft Power BI**: Used to create interactive dashboards and visualize business insights.
- **Power Query**: Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preprocessing.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**: Used to create calculated columns, measures, and business KPIs.
- **Power BI Data Modeling**: Used to establish relationships between Customers, Products, Orders, and Order Items tables.
- **Data Visualization**: Used to represent business data through charts, graphs, tables, and KPI cards.

## Business Insights
- Identified the top-performing product categories based on revenue contribution.
- Analyzed monthly sales trends to understand changes in business performance over time.
- Identified the products generating the highest and lowest sales.
- Analyzed customer purchasing patterns to identify one-time and repeat customers.
- Identified high-value customers based on their total spending and purchase frequency.
- Evaluated city-wise customer distribution and revenue contribution.

## Recommendations
- Focus marketing campaigns on high-performing product categories to increase overall revenue.
- Introduce loyalty programs and special discounts to encourage repeat purchases.
- Provide personalized offers to high-value customers to improve customer retention.
- Promote low-performing products through discounts and targeted marketing campaigns.
- Maintain sufficient inventory for high-demand products to avoid stock shortages.
-Investigate the reasons behind cancelled orders and take steps to reduce cancellation rates.
